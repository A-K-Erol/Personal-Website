I'm a BS/MS student at Georgia Tech (GPA 4.0, graduating May 2026) studying Computer Science, with a focus on the challenges of deploying real-time, high-throughput software systems at scale — for analytical, machine learning, and transaction processing workloads. I am also a Graduate Teaching Assistant for **CS 3600: Artificial Intelligence**.

My research at the **GT System Infrastructure and Architecture Lab** (advised by [Dr. Divya Mahajan](https://sites.google.com/view/divyamahajan)) centers on scaling ML inference on constrained and distributed systems. I have presented this work as first author at **MLSys '25 and '26** and at the **CRNCH Summit '26**, and received the **President's Undergraduate Research Award (PURA)**. I am a **National Merit Scholar** and **Faculty Honors** recipient.

On the industry side, I interned at **Google** (Mountain View, Summer 2025), where I optimized gRPC networking for a low-latency C++ inference system in private ML ad infrastructure — cutting deployment costs by $800k/yr and boosting model serving throughput by 1.6x. Before that, I interned at **Travelers Insurance** on cloud data infrastructure. I am an incoming **Forward Deployed Software Engineer at [Baseten](https://www.baseten.co/)** (San Francisco, starting June 2026).

I am also a Board Member and former Finance Director of [People for PSEO](https://www.peopleforpseo.org/), a Minnesota non-profit advocating for dual-enrollment students, where I led grant and donor initiatives resulting in 2.3x budget growth.

I am a native speaker of both **English** and **Turkish**.

Outside of work and research, I enjoy [classical music](/passions/#music), [travel](/passions/#travel), [pastry](/passions/#food), and [poetry](/passions/#poetry) — visit my [passions page](/passions/) to learn more.
