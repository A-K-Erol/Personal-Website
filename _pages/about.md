I'm a BS/MS student at Georgia Tech (graduated May 2026) studying Computer Science, with a focus on deploying real-time, high-throughput software systems at scale — for analytical, machine learning, and transaction processing workloads. I am a Forward Deployed Engineer at Baseten, where I focus on production ML deployment and model-serving infrastructure. I am also a Graduate Teaching Assistant for CS 3600: Artificial Intelligence.

My research at the [Systems for Scalable Intelligence (SCAI) Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/divyamahajan) (advised by Dr. Divya Mahajan) centers on scaling ML inference on constrained and distributed systems — efficient MoE model serving and edge computing on satellites. I have presented first-author work at [MLSys](https://mlsys.org/) '25 and '26, and received the **Georgia Tech Outstanding MS Research Award** and the [Marshall D. Williamson Fellowship](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/college-computing-annual-awards-and-honors) for academic excellence and my effectiveness as a teaching assistant.

On the industry side, I previously worked at **Google** (Mountain View, Summer 2025), where I optimized gRPC networking for a low-latency C++ inference system in private ML ad infrastructure. I am a Forward Deployed Engineer at Baseten (San Francisco). I am also a Board Member and former Finance Director of [People for PSEO](https://www.peopleforpseo.org/), a Minnesota non-profit advocating for dual-enrollment students.

Outside of work and research, I enjoy [classical music](/passions/#music), [travel](/passions/#travel), [pastry](/passions/#food), and [poetry](/passions/#poetry) — visit my [passions page](/passions/) to learn more.
